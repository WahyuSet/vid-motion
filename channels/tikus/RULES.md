# Channel Si Tikus — rules (politik, pemerintahan, keuangan, investasi)

Mascot **Si Tikus** (`tikus`, soft plush style), theme **newsprint** (newspaper grey, condensed news titles,
lower-third captions). Channel name and handle are not chosen yet (`channel.json`).

These topics carry legal and reputational risk. The rules below are stricter than `AGENTS.md` and win over it.

## Accuracy and sources
- Only **primary or official sources** for facts and numbers: BPS, Bank Indonesia, OJK, Kemenkeu, KPU, Bawaslu,
  DPR/JDIH (the text of a law or regulation), court rulings, official statements, plus established news outlets
  (Kompas, Tempo, Antara, CNBC Indonesia, Katadata) to report *what happened*. Show the source with `cite` on
  screen every time a number or claim appears, and give the date ("data BPS, Agustus 2026").
- Separate **fakta** (what happened, what a rule says) from **opini/analisis**. Say "menurut …" for claims.
- News moves fast: state the date of the information in the script or on screen; never present an old number as current.

## Politics and government
- **Non-partisan.** Explain how things work (how a law is passed, what a budget line means, what a policy changes);
  never endorse or attack a party, candidate or official. Give the main sides of a debate fairly.
- **No accusations** against identifiable people or organisations beyond what a court or official finding states;
  "diduga" claims need a named, reputable source. No insults, nicknames or mockery of real people.
- Do **not** use the mascot or the word "tikus" as a joke about corruption or about any real person or office
  (in Indonesia "tikus" is slang for a corrupt official); Si Tikus is a friendly explainer.
- No content that could mislead about elections (dates, places, how to vote); during campaign periods prefer
  procedural explainers from KPU/Bawaslu sources.
- No SARA framing.

## Money and investment
- **Education only**: concepts (inflasi, bunga majemuk, diversifikasi, risiko), how products work, how to check
  legality. Never recommend buying/selling a specific stock, coin or product, never predict prices, never promise returns.
- Every finance video ends with, and its description includes: *"Konten edukasi, bukan ajakan investasi.
  Cek legalitas produk di OJK (ojk.go.id / Kontak 157)."*
- Warn against schemes that promise fixed high returns (investasi bodong) with OJK / Satgas PASTI as the source.

## Formats
- `jelasin` (explain a policy or concept in 45–60 s), `cek fakta` (a viral claim → verdict stamp `FAKTA`/`HOAKS`/
  `MENYESATKAN` → evidence), `angka penting` (one official number explained with a local comparison).
- Si Tikus faces: `skeptical`/`confused` for the viral claim, `smug` + stamp for the verdict, `sad`/`shock`
  for risks, `smile` for the takeaway. Never `laugh` at a person.
