# Channel Sejarah — rules (sejarah Indonesia, tempat, tokoh, tradisi)

No mascot. Theme **arsip** (aged archive paper, sepia, serif titles on paper strips, typewriter captions, an
oxblood archive stamp). The picture is carried by realistic props drawn in code: gradients, soft blurred
shadows, highlights (old maps, coins, letters with seals, quill and ink, compass, ships, lanterns, spice sacks,
sepia photo frames, a timeline ribbon). Channel name and handle are not chosen yet (`channel.json`).

These rules are stricter than `AGENTS.md` and win over it.

## Accuracy and sources
- Sources, best first: ANRI, Perpusnas, museums and cagar budaya records (Kemendikbud), peer-reviewed journals
  and history books, keraton or official records, Britannica; established news (Kompas, Tempo, Detik, Antara,
  The Jakarta Post) to report what happened or what someone stated. Write every claim with its source in
  `sources.md`.
- Every year and date appears on screen (headline, card or `cite`). When sources give different years, either
  say "menurut …" or leave the year out.
- Separate the record from the story: legends, oral tradition and beliefs are said as such ("menurut cerita
  turun-temurun", "dipercaya sebagian peziarah"), never as fact.

## Sensitive history and belief
- Neutral on contested history (for example 1965, regional conflicts): give the main accounts with their
  sources, take no side, no blame on groups.
- No SARA framing. Religious sites and practices are described with respect: what people do and why, in their
  own words or a caretaker's; never mock, never endorse supernatural claims (pesugihan, kesaktian).
- No glorifying violence or war; no graphic depiction of death or injury.
- Real people are shown as silhouettes, statues, signatures or objects linked to them, never as an invented
  face or a copied photograph.

## Formats
- `jejak` (the story of one place or object), `asal-usul` (where a name, word or custom comes from),
  `tokoh` (one person's role, with dates), `mitos sejarah` (a popular belief → stamp `TERCATAT` / `BELUM
  TERBUKTI` → the record).
- Stamps use the seal style of the theme: `TERCATAT`, `ARSIP`, `BELUM TERBUKTI`, `MENURUT CERITA`.

## Real archive images (exception to "everything is drawn" in AGENTS.md)
- This channel may use real photos, maps, paintings and documents, but only with a clear reuse license:
  public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA (Rijksmuseum, Leiden/KITLV, Nationaal Archief, Wikimedia Commons,
  Library of Congress, own photos). Never images found through a search engine without a license.
- Ask the human before downloading: list each file with its title, source page, author, license and size.
- Keep files in `topics/<id>/assets/` and list every one in `topics/<id>/assets.md` (file, what it shows,
  source URL, author, license, credit needed).
- Treat them to fit the arsip look (sepia, deckle-edged print, tape, slow zoom) and caption every print with
  what and when it shows, plus a credit line on screen; CC BY / CC BY-SA credits also go in the description,
  and an adapted CC BY-SA image stays under CC BY-SA.
- Caption only what the source says the image shows; never present a photo of one place as another.
- Real images support the story; drawn props, cards and thread carry it, so the video is not a slideshow.
