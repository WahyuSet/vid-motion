# Cicak Tahu — channel rules

Mascot **Si Cicak** (`cicak`), theme **paper**. Topics: fakta unik, mitos atau fakta, everyday science,
nature and animals. Tone: playful, curious, casual Indonesian.

- Formats: `mitos-fakta` (belief → verdict stamp → mechanism) and `fakta-unik` (one surprising fact explained).
- Out of scope: politics, government, religion, ethnicity (SARA), money/investment advice, personal medical
  advice. Health myths are fine as general information with a "periksa ke dokter" line when relevant.
- The general rules in `AGENTS.md` (sources, numbers, loop ending, local references) all apply.
