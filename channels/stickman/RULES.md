# Channel Stickman — rules (kesehatan, tubuh manusia, edukasi)

Mascot **Stickman** (`stickman`, doodle style), theme **notebook** (lined page, handwriting, highlighter).
Channel name and handle are not chosen yet (`channel.json`). The audience includes students: keep it clear,
friendly and safe for all ages.

These rules are stricter than `AGENTS.md` and win over it.

## Health
- Sources: WHO, Kemenkes RI, CDC, NIH/MedlinePlus, NHS, IDAI (children), peer-reviewed reviews, medical
  textbooks/encyclopedias. Show the source with `cite` for every number or health claim.
- **General information only.** No diagnosis, no treatment or dosage instructions, no "obat ini menyembuhkan".
  When symptoms come up, say to see a doctor or puskesmas. Emergencies: mention 119 / IGD.
- Follow YouTube's medical misinformation policy: never contradict WHO / health-authority guidance
  (vaccines, treatments, how diseases spread).
- No fear-mongering, no body shaming, no weight-loss or supplement promotion. Mental-health topics: gentle tone
  and a pointer to professional help (e.g. layanan kesehatan jiwa / psikolog).
- Health videos' descriptions end with: *"Informasi umum, bukan pengganti saran dokter."*

## Human body and education
- Explain one mechanism per video with a simple drawing (organ, cell, process) and one memorable comparison.
- Use correct terms, then a plain-word explanation ("alveolus, kantong udara kecil di paru-paru").
- Numbers rounded honestly with "sekitar"; state ranges when they vary by age or person.
- Drawings of the body stay schematic and non-graphic (no gore, no realistic injuries).

## Formats
- `kok bisa` (how the body does something), `mitos kesehatan` (belief → stamp `MITOS`/`FAKTA` → evidence),
  `catatan belajar` (a school-topic concept explained like class notes).
- Stickman faces: `confused` for the question, `think` pose while explaining, `wow` for the surprising number,
  `smug` + stamp for verdicts, `happy`/`laugh` at the end.
