# sumber — serat-jeruk

Satu bullet per klaim: klaim → kalimat persis dari sumber → URL.

- **Orang dewasa butuh minimal 25 gram serat alami per hari** — WHO: "Everyone older than 10 years of age
  should also aim for a daily intake of at least 25 grams of naturally-occurring dietary fibre as found in
  foods, with lesser amounts for children under 10: at least 15 or 21 grams for children 2–5 or 6–9 years of
  age, respectively." — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet (fact sheet 26 Jan 2026).
- **Daging jeruk mentah: 2,4 gram serat per 100 g** — USDA FoodData Central, "Oranges, raw, all commercial
  varieties", nutrient 291 (Total dietary fiber) = 2.4 g/100 g —
  https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-app#/food-details/169097/nutrients.
- **Jus jeruk mentah: 0,2 gram serat per 100 g** — USDA FoodData Central, "Orange juice, raw", nutrient 291 =
  0.2 g/100 g — https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-app#/food-details/169098/nutrients. (0,2 → 12× lebih kecil dari
  2,4; itu turunan aritmetika dari dua angka USDA di atas.)
- **Serat larut membentuk gel → pencernaan melambat, gula naik lebih pelan, kenyang lebih lama** — Harvard
  T. H. Chan School: "Some types of soluble fibers… have a gel-like quality… Fiber helps regulate the body's
  use of sugars"; "Soluble fiber attracts water in the gut, forming a gel, which can slow digestion. This may
  help prevent blood glucose surges after eating and reduce hunger." —
  https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/carbohydrates/fiber/
  (sumber kedua:) Wikipedia/Dietary fiber, dikutip EFSA-bahasa-efek serat viskos: "Viscous fiber… delays
  gastric emptying, which in humans can result in an extended feeling of fullness." —
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dietary_fiber.
- **Serat tak larut → kelancaran pencernaan (regularity)** — Harvard: "Insoluble fiber, which does not
  dissolve in water, can help food move through your digestive system, promoting regularity and helping
  prevent constipation." — https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/carbohydrates/fiber/
  (sumber kedua:) Wikipedia/Dietary fiber mengutip mekanisme: "Bulking fibers… absorb and hold water,
  promoting bowel movement regularity." — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dietary_fiber.
- **Pektin ada di jeruk (bagian putih/kulitnya)** — Wikipedia/Orange (fruit): "The entire fruit is cut up and
  boiled with sugar; the pith contributes pectin, which helps the marmalade to set." —
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_(fruit). Sifat pektin = serat larut:
  Harvard: "Pectins – Soluble highly fermentable fiber found in apples, berries, and other fruits."
- **Makan buah utuh > jus untuk serat** — Harvard tips: "Eat whole fruits instead of drinking fruit juices." —
  https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/carbohydrates/fiber/
  (sumber kedua:) WHO: "While fruit juice can be consumed, most varieties including those without added
  sugars contain significant amounts of free sugars, the consumption of which should be limited." —
  https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet.
