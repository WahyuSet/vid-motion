# Sumber: Kaktus bisa hidup di gurun tanpa air?

Format: mitos-fakta. Jawaban: **mitos**. Kaktus tetap butuh air, tapi sangat pandai menyimpan dan menghematnya.

| Klaim di naskah | Kutipan / angka dari sumber | Sumber |
|---|---|---|
| Kaktus tetap butuh air; akar dangkal menangkap hujan | "As with most desert plants, cacti have a shallow root system … enable cacti to capture the rainwater from even the lightest showers." | NPS, Saguaro National Park, *Cacti and Desert Succulents* — https://home.nps.gov/sagu/learn/nature/cacti-and-desert-succulents.htm |
| Akar sekitar 7 cm di bawah permukaan, menyebar sejauh tinggi kaktus | "a network of roots that lies about three inches below the desert surface and stretches as far from the main trunk as saguaros are tall" (3 inci ≈ 7,6 cm) | NPS, brosur Saguaro National Park (2010) — https://www.npshistory.com/publications/sagu/brochures/2010.pdf |
| Lipatan batang mengembang saat menyerap air, menyusut saat dipakai | "These pleats allow the plant to expand while it is absorbing rainwater … shrink when it is using its stores of water." / "Accordion-like pleats allow the cactus to expand and hold water" | NPS *Cacti and Desert Succulents*; brosur NPS 2010 |
| Saguaro bisa menyerap sampai ~750 liter sekali hujan, cukup untuk setahun | "may soak up as much as 200 gallons of water, enough to last a saguaro for a year" (200 galon AS ≈ 757 L) | Brosur NPS 2010; juga High Country News: "store as much as 200 gallons — 1,668 pounds — of water" — https://www.hcn.org/issues/57-1/what-an-ancient-saguaro-can-teach-us/ |
| ≈ 40 galon air minum 19 L | 750 L ÷ 19 L ≈ 39,5 (perhitungan sendiri) | — |
| Duri = daun yang berubah bentuk; meneduhkan, menahan angin kering | "the spines, which are modified leaves" / "spines provide shade during hot summer days" / "help prevent water loss that occurs due to dry winds" | NPS *Cacti and Desert Succulents* |
| Pori-pori (stomata) membuka di malam hari | Cacti use Crassulacean Acid Metabolism; pores "open only at night when desert temperatures are lower and humidities are higher" | NPS *Cacti and Desert Succulents* |
| Kebanyakan disiram → akar busuk | "one of the most common problems we find in samples … is caused by overwatering"; water when the soil is dry | Texas A&M Plant Clinic, *Soft Spot for Succulents* — https://plantclinic.tamu.edu/2019/01/25/soft-spot-for-succulents/ ; Iowa State Univ.
