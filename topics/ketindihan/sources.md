# ketindihan — sources

Topik: penjelasan sains untuk pengalaman "gaib" sehari-hari: ketindihan (kelumpuhan tidur) dan pareidolia.
Format `kok-bisa`. Video ini menjelaskan **pengalamannya**, tidak membahas ada atau tidaknya makhluk gaib
(menghindari wilayah keyakinan, sesuai RULES.md Cicak Tahu).

| klaim di naskah | bukti (kutipan / angka) | sumber |
|---|---|---|
| Bangun tapi tidak bisa bergerak, terasa ada sosok di kamar | "when you cannot move or speak as you are waking up or falling asleep" · gejala: "like someone is in your room", "like something is pushing you down" | NHS, Sleep paralysis |
| Sains menyebutnya kelumpuhan tidur (sleep paralysis) | nama kondisi | NHS; Cleveland Clinic |
| Saat bermimpi, otak mengunci otot supaya tidak ikut bergerak | "Your brain prevents the muscles in your limbs from moving to protect yourself from acting dreams out" · "muscle atonia — helps you remain still during REM sleep" | Cleveland Clinic, Sleep paralysis |
| Kadang sudah sadar duluan, tapi kuncinya belum lepas | "Sleep paralysis happens when you regain awareness going into or coming out of rapid eye movement (REM) sleep." | Cleveland Clinic |
| Halusinasi: sosok, suara, dada terasa berat | "Sensations of pressure against your chest" · "Hallucinations (like there's a dangerous person in your room)" | Cleveland Clinic; NHS |
| Sekitar 8 dari 100 orang pernah mengalaminya | prevalensi seumur hidup "7.6% of the general population" (36.533 peserta) | Sharpless & Barber (2011), Sleep Medicine Reviews 15(5):311–315 |
| Melihat wajah di benda (tembok, jemuran, stopkontak) itu pareidolia | "we sometimes perceive illusory faces in objects" | Wardle dkk. (2020), Nature Communications 11:4518 |
| Otak jago mencari wajah, benda pun terlihat seperti muka | "The human brain is specialized for face processing" · "Illusory faces are initially represented more similarly to real faces than matched objects are" | Wardle dkk. (2020) |
| Tidak berbahaya; tidur cukup; ke dokter kalau sering dan mengganggu | "it can be scary but it's harmless" · tidur 7–9 jam teratur · ke dokter kalau sering dan takut tidur atau selalu lelah | NHS |
