# Sumber: golongan-darah

- **Ada 4 golongan darah utama: A, B, AB, O.** NHS: "There are 4 main blood groups (types of blood) – A, B, AB and O." MedlinePlus: "The 4 major blood types are: A, B, AB, O."
  https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/blood-groups/ · https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003345.htm
- **Bedanya ada di antigen, penanda (protein) di permukaan sel darah merah.** NHS: "Antigens are protein molecules found on the surface of red blood cells." MedlinePlus: "Your blood type is based on whether or not certain proteins are on your red blood cells."
- **A punya antigen A, B punya antigen B, AB punya keduanya, O tidak punya.** NHS (definisi tiap golongan; AB: "both A and B antigens", O: "no antigens").
- **Rhesus positif/negatif → 8 golongan.** NHS: "Red blood cells sometimes have another antigen, a protein known as the RhD antigen … This means you can be 1 of 8 blood groups." MedlinePlus: Rh+ jika punya protein ini, Rh− jika tidak.
- **Salah transfusi berbahaya; antibodi menyerang sel darah asing.** NHS: "Receiving blood from the wrong ABO group can be life-threatening." MedlinePlus: "Your immune system will see the donated red blood cells as foreign … Antibodies will … attack these blood cells."
- **O negatif bisa diberikan ke hampir semua orang saat darurat.** NHS: "In most cases, O RhD negative blood (O-) can safely be given to anyone. It's often used in medical emergencies when the blood type is not immediately known." MedlinePlus: "Type O blood can be given to anyone with any blood type."
- **Di Indonesia, rhesus negatif kurang dari 1 persen.** Reswari, Amalia & Primasari (2022), *Journal of Community Health Development* 3(2):16–22, mengutip BPS: "in 2010 the rhesus negative population in Indonesia did not reach 1% (about 1.2 million people)". Data Pusdatin Kemenkes 2016 (dikutip jurnal unjaya) menyebut 0,1%. Kedua angka konsisten dengan "kurang dari satu persen".
  https://jos.unsoed.ac.id/index.php/jchd/article/view/4806 · https://ejournal.unjaya.ac.id/index.php/mik/article/download/744/625
- **Golongan darah diturunkan dari orang tua.** NHS: "Your blood group is determined by the genes you inherit from your parents."

Tidak dipakai: "AB+ resipien universal" (NHS tidak menyebutnya), persentase ABO nasional (tidak ada data nasional resmi yang konsisten).
