# cicak-nempel — sources

Topik: kenapa cicak (dan tokek) bisa nempel di langit-langit tanpa jatuh. Format `mitos-fakta`.
Studi utama dilakukan pada **tokek** (*Gekko gecko*, tokay gecko); angka di naskah disebut untuk tokek.

| klaim di naskah | bukti (kutipan / angka) | sumber |
|---|---|---|
| Kaki cicak tidak berlem; tidak ada kelenjar lem | "gecko feet do not have glue glands" · "there are no glue glands on the foot, nor is glue residue left on the surface" | Scientific American 2000; UC Berkeley 2000 |
| Bukan mengisap (bukan suction); tetap nempel di ruang hampa | "ruled out both friction and suction as mechanisms of gecko grip" · "gecko feet appear to work quite well on glass and in vacuums" · "suction is much less effective than the measured sticking force" | Scientific American 2000; UC Berkeley 2000 |
| Tokek punya sekitar dua juta bulu super halus (setae) di jarinya | "two million microscopic hairs on their toes" (UC Berkeley); sejalan dengan "about half a million" setae per kaki (Scientific American) × 4 kaki | UC Berkeley 2000; Scientific American 2000 |
| Ujung tiap bulu bercabang jadi ratusan bantalan mungil (spatulae) | "the hundreds to thousands of tiny pads at the tip of each hair" · abstract Nature: setae end in "hundreds of projections" (spatulae 0,2–0,5 µm) | UC Berkeley 2000; Autumn et al., Nature 2000 |
| Bantalan begitu dekat ke permukaan sehingga molekulnya saling tarik: gaya van der Waals | "Adhesive force values support the hypothesis that individual seta operate by van der Waals forces" · PNAS 2002: bukti langsung adhesi van der Waals, bukan kapiler | Autumn et al., Nature 2000; Autumn et al., PNAS 2002 |
| Satu bulu lemah, jutaan bulu kuat | "a single hair could lift an ant" · "a million hairs ... could lift a small child of about 45 pounds" | UC Berkeley 2000 |
| Sejuta bulu seluas koin bisa menahan sekitar 20 kg, seberat anak kecil | "a million hairs covering an area the size of a dime could lift a small child of about 45 pounds" (45 lb ≈ 20,4 kg) | UC Berkeley 2000 / ScienceDaily 2000 |
| Untuk lepas, cicak menggulung (mengelupas) jarinya ke atas | "If the hair is levered upward at a 30 degree angle ... the spatulae ... easily detach. The gecko does this simply by peeling its toes off the surface." | UC Berkeley 2000 / ScienceDaily 2000 |

Catatan: kelembapan udara ikut menambah daya rekat (bulu melunak), tapi van der Waals tetap mekanisme utama
yang didukung bukti (Autumn et al.). Klaim kelembapan tidak dipakai di naskah.
