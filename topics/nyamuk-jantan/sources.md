# Sumber: nyamuk-jantan

Satu poin per klaim: klaim dalam bahasa Indonesia, kutipan asli dari sumber resmi, dan URL.

- **Hanya nyamuk betina yang menggigit manusia untuk mengisap darah; nyamuk jantan tidak minum darah.**
  CDC: "Only female mosquitoes bite people and animals to get a blood meal." dan "The male's proboscis is not strong enough to pierce skin, and males do not feed on blood."
  https://www.cdc.gov/mosquitoes/about/
- **Makanan nyamuk jantan (dan betina untuk energi harian) adalah nektar bunga dan sari buah.**
  CDC: "Proboscis: Both female and male mosquitoes use the proboscis to feed on flower nectar and fruit juices."
  Wikipedia: "All mosquitoes drink nectar from flowers; females of many species have adapted to also drink blood."
  https://www.cdc.gov/mosquitoes/about/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito
- **Jarum mulut (proboscis) nyamuk jantan tidak cukup kuat menembus kulit manusia, sedangkan betina punya 6 jarum penusuk (stylets) di dalam selubung proboscis.**
  CDC: "In female mosquitoes, this mouth part pierces the skin of a person or animal and sucks out blood. The male's proboscis is not strong enough to pierce skin, and males do not feed on blood."
  Wikipedia: "Externally, the most obvious feeding structure of the mosquito is the proboscis, composed of the labium, U-shaped in section like a rain gutter, which sheaths a bundle (fascicle) of six piercing mouthparts or stylets."
  https://www.cdc.gov/mosquitoes/about/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito
- **Nyamuk betina membutuhkan protein dari darah untuk mematangkan telurnya (sekitar 100 sampai 200 telur selama hidupnya).**
  CDC: "Female mosquitoes need a blood meal to produce eggs."
  Wikipedia: "Acquiring a blood meal (protein) is essential for egg production" dan "A female can lay 100–200 eggs in her lifetime."
  https://www.cdc.gov/mosquitoes/about/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito
- **Antena nyamuk jantan tampak lebat berbulu (bushy), sedangkan antena betina lebih tipis.**
  Wikipedia: "Male (left) has bushy antennae and longer palps than female (right)."
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito
- **Virus demam berdarah (DBD) ditularkan oleh gigitan nyamuk betina (terutama Aedes aegypti), dan telur diletakkan di air menggenang.**
  WHO: "The dengue virus is transmitted to humans through the bites of infected female mosquitoes, primarily the Aedes aegypti mosquito."
  Wikipedia: "The first three stages (egg, larva, and pupa) are largely aquatic, the eggs usually being laid in stagnant water."
  https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/dengue-and-severe-dengue · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito
